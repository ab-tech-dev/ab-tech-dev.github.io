# ab-tech-dev revamp brief

Compiled from the existing conversation, with authored design decisions marked below. This is an implementation brief, not an invented interview.

## Evidence and direction

- User: "i want you to use all these skills to revamp the entire website".
- Hero preference: "The recent three-stage hero, before the last positioning fixes". Preserve its three acts, type, split composition, bracket geometry and desktop timeline. Keep text accessible on mobile.
- Previous request: planned, related transitions, parallax and 3D across the website. Aim for a distinctive studio experience.
- Subject: ab-tech-dev, independent software, automation and applied AI studio. Audience inferred from existing copy: business owners needing custom products and connected operations.
- Existing assets: bracket wordmark, lime/forest palette, Three.js scene, source content and section destinations. These are retained.
- Authored vibe: precise, dimensional, confident, human.
- Authored journey: possibility in the hero; recognition in the studio statement; control in a working service demonstration; confidence in the delivery process; readiness to start a conversation.
- Authored energy: hero 8, studio 3, expertise 6, approach 4, contact 5, footer 2. The established hero assembly is the visual peak and retains the longest scroll span.
- Authored signature: hand a sample enquiry to a connected workflow, pause at human approval, and complete the handoff yourself. A local interactive example, never an assertion of a real integration.
- Authored grammar: dimensional studio portfolio with a working demonstration. Existing section navigation and fixed brand scene tie it together. It is not a camera flight through a fictional world.
- Authored art direction: real-time brand geometry and operable HTML; no stock portraits, fabricated clients, invented performance statistics or generated dashboard pictures.

## Feeling curve, before the score

Hero: possibility, the separated work panels assemble into one system.
Studio: recognition, scattered tools are reframed as one connected working day.
Expertise: control, changing a service changes an operable example.
Approach: confidence, deliverables become concrete as the process advances.
Contact: readiness, a direct invitation resolves into an email action.
Footer: calm, the bracket signature returns to the brand.

Peak: "The scattered work came together into one system." Hero, third act.
Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where you can move an enquiry through a workflow and decide when it is approved."
Editorial breathing room separates the three hero acts, while every pinned interval still changes the scene or advances comprehension.

## Tokens and layout plan

Colors: forest #101713, raised forest #19251e, mineral #26372d, paper ink #eff1e9, muted sage #a4aea1, brand lime #c4f975. Colors extracted from source, not estimated from screenshots.
Typography: original Arial hero preserved; self-hosted Manrope 400/600 for the rest. Display 48-112px, body 16-18px, controls 14-16px. No mixed serif emphasis.
Shape: 24px main workbench, 12px internal controls, pill primary action retained from brand. No decorative section counters.

[ large studio statement / quiet explanation / small connected motif ]
[ expertise title ]
[ service selector rail | operable demonstration and service description ]
[ process heading and scene | three staggered deliverable chapters ]
[ oversized invitation / direct email action ]
[ signature and wordmark / compact navigation ]

Review: repeating pale/dark sections, numbered eyebrow headers, identical slide-up entrances and static dashboard paintings were the weak points. Replace them with a consistent dark canvas, different section compositions, focused motion and working samples. Keep the hero as requested, even where generic skill preferences would remove its existing cues or accent words.

## Scroll score

Global stage: a fixed Three.js system, grain, veil, progress line and chapter readout connect the page as one continuous sequence.
Hero: one pinned three-act scene on desktop and mobile; deliberate pauses separate Imagine, Connect and Create before the page releases into Studio.
Studio: concentric depth field, line-by-line convergence and a restrained connection motif.
Expertise: a migrating grid and orbit field frame the perspective arrival of a stable workbench; service selection crossfades and enquiry state responds to visitor input.
Approach: the shared system becomes a process engine while steps arrive from alternating depth positions and their rails fill with progress.
Contact: corridor lines and a portal-like scene support two converging headline planes, followed by a direct actionable close.
Footer: the geometry contracts into a centered signature while the wordmark rises and settles.

## Tool adaptation

Taste and redesign skills govern hierarchy and consistency; attached Frontend Design governs specific content and restraint; Design DNA records code-derived identity; Scroll Craft supplies journey, device variety and verification.
